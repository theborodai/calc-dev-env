A product choice the PO hasn't explicitly answered must be asked in its own PO Issue (§11 format), not recorded as a "team interpretation the PO can object to in the PR".

**Why:** 2026-11-05 I proposed writing "evaluation only on a fully resolved BOM" into DEC.055 as team interpretation, assuming PO would see it in the PR; user: "РО по факту не дивиться в ПР", ask explicitly in an issue.

**How to apply:** for any unanswered product semantics in a DEC draft → separate PO Issue; DEC lists it as open question until answered. Related: [[po-questions-separate-issues]], [[no-pending-promises-in-issues]].
